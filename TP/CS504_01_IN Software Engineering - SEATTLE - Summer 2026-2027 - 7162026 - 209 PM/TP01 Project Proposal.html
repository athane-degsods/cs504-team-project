For the first part of the project, complete the following tasks: 

  1. Identify four multi-factor authentication systems currently available (i.e., your school login uses Microsoft's Azure Active Director Multi-Factor Authentication). 

  1. Explain the functionality of those three systems. Please show examples of how to use them that, include print-screens 

  1. Compare the different systems' functionality and identify your group's preferred system.  

 

The report for part 1 should contain the following: 

  1. An Overview of multi-factor authentication 

  1. The mechanism the group used to select the four examples 

  1. The four examples and their functionality 

  1. The comparison of the four examples
