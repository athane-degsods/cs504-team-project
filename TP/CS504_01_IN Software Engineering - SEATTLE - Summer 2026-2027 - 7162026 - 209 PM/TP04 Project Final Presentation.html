Team Project Final Presentation

  • Final PPT (15+4 slides, 30 points; graded after the final report has been submitted) - Starting (Module 8) & Ending (Module 10)  

The student will report on the research outcomes, development, or other project efforts to an academically appropriate committee in a public forum. 

Each team will have 15 minutes for presentation and 5 minutes for questions and answers.  Each team must keep the total presentation time limit strictly.  

For online students, your team can choose to embed the audio into individual slides or you can record the presentation and submit a video link along with your presentation file. The length should be no longer than 15 minutes and there will not be a Q&A.

The presentation should include a demonstration of your application. This can be done using a series of screenshots or by imbedding a video showing the application's functionality.
